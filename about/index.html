What I care about

Big-company tools for small businesses

Most of my career has gone into tools for people running a business: a hotel manager setting tomorrow's rates, a car dealer deciding what a car is worth, a seller listing stock in another country.

These businesses rarely have an analyst, an engineer or a pricing team. A large operator takes that kind of capability for granted. Giving a small business the same thing, in a form it can use between everything else it has to do, is the problem I keep coming back to.

It's also why I like B2B and SaaS work. The users are experts in their own trade, they notice when something wastes their time, and a good tool shows up directly in how their business runs.

The design leaders I've learned most from were the ones still doing the work.

How I lead

The work and the people doing it

At AutoScout24 I lead the design team for our dealer-facing products. I split my time between the standard of what the team ships and the people shipping it.

  • I coach, and I give direct feedback when performance needs to change.
  • I hire with a high bar. I've run interviews, portfolio reviews and whiteboard sessions for more than ten design roles.
  • I clear the organisational blockers that stall good work, and I make sure design has a voice in the rooms where decisions get made.
  • I keep ownership of the design system, and I take on the hardest design problems myself.
The kind of role I look for

One that asks for both leadership and hands-on design. That combination is rare, and it's the one I want.

How I got here

From web design to design leadership

I studied Innovative Product Design at the University of Dundee, then started out freelancing on websites. E-commerce at a whisky distiller came next. At Arnold Clark I led a team of five designers and built the company's first design system.

At Booking.com I designed revenue and operations tools for hotel partners. I built the BookingSuite App Store from scratch as its only designer, and later worked on guest check-in and the Connected Trip strategy. Since 2021 I've been at AutoScout24, first as Lead and Principal Designer, and now as Team Lead.
