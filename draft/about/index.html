What I'm interested in

Three things on my desk

B2B tools

Business users are experts in their trade, and they notice when a tool wastes their time. I love the close feedback loop, and giving business users the kind of quality experience consumers usually expect.

AI workflows and design systems

UX teams are transforming through AI, but it's easy to lose what makes us important: user focus. Working with my UX team, I'm excited about exploring how these systems can superpower a design team, not replace it.

Running teams

Good teams need a high bar, honest feedback and someone clearing the way. I coach, hire and remove blockers, and I love still getting my hands dirty now and then.
