Chapter 1. Context

Same car, different price

Car prices vary a lot across Europe. Italy had a surplus of EVs while Denmark couldn't get enough, so dealers could profit by selling stock across the border to other dealers.

I led design with autoproff, a B2B auction platform, alongside a PM, engineers, a researcher and a content designer, to make that as easy as listing a car.

Chapter 2. Problem

One more marketplace to manage

Dealers' stock already lived on AutoScout24. Selling it to other dealers meant listing it again on another platform, and every extra marketplace adds work to manage.

Research also showed something that would matter later: dealers wanted to choose to take part, not be signed up by default.

Chapter 3. What I did

Publish once, sell across borders

I designed a flow that publishes a dealer's existing AutoScout24 listings straight to autoproff, so there's no second marketplace to manage.

The call

Launch opt-out, against the research.

The business wanted to test whether dealers would sell their consumer stock to other dealers if the demand was there. But without that demand, it would be hard to convince dealers to change how they sell. Opt-out broke the chicken-and-egg problem: fill the marketplace first, and let demand follow.

Chapter 4. Results

Supply worked. Demand didn't.

Thousands of listings arrived quickly, from dealers across Italy and Germany. But many of those dealers had been signed up by default rather than choosing to sell, and offers went unanswered.

A marketplace full of listings, with dealers who don't reply, isn't a marketplace. It's a catalogue.

Chapter 5. Next steps

Research, vindicated. Mostly.

The platform has since moved to opt-in. Inactive dealers were cleared out at the same time, and seller response jumped the following week. I'd moved to a new role by then.

Because both changes landed together, neither can be isolated. Offer volume fell as the dealer base shrank, and while response improved, sales didn't follow.

Opt-in fixed the problem the research predicted, and revealed another underneath: a responsive dealer isn't automatically a buying one.
