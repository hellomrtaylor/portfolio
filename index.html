Three things on my desk

Marketplaces

I love the puzzle of a product where buyers and sellers both need to win. At AutoScout24 and Booking.com I’ve designed for both sides, from people looking for their next car or hotel to the dealers and hoteliers serving them, in markets all over Europe.

AI workflows and design systems

UX teams are transforming through AI, but it's easy to lose what makes us important: user focus. Working with my UX team, I'm excited about exploring how these systems can superpower a design team, not replace it.

Raising the bar

I raise the bar by working alongside designers, not above them: pairing on hard problems, giving honest critique and setting standards that make good work the default.
